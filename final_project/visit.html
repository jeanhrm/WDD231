Visit Huancavelica

Huancavelica is located in Peru’s central highlands. Because of altitude and weather changes, visitors should bring warm layers, sun protection, and stay hydrated.

Quick Travel Tips

  • Altitude: Take it slow on the first day and drink plenty of water.
  • Weather: Bring a jacket, rain protection, and sunscreen.
  • Transportation: Plan routes early and confirm schedules when possible.
  • Respect culture: Ask before taking photos in communities or festivals.
