Discover Huancavelica — Site Plan

Three-page informational website showcasing Huancavelica (Peru) as a tourism destination through culture, nature, and heritage.

Site Name Purpose Scenarios Color Scheme Typography Wireframes

Site Name

Discover Huancavelica

Optional domain idea: discoverhuancavelica.com (optional)

This name clearly communicates the goal of the site: helping visitors explore Huancavelica’s identity, culture, and natural landscapes through a tourism-focused experience.

Planned Pages

  • Home: Introduction to Huancavelica and its identity.
  • Attractions: Key places, landscapes, heritage, and cultural elements.
  • Visit: Basic travel information, recommendations, and planning tips.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote Huancavelica (Peru) as a tourism destination by presenting its cultural heritage, natural landscapes, and historical places. The site will offer inspiring and practical information about attractions (mountains, lagoons, festivals, and heritage) and provide basic recommendations to help visitors plan a visit.

Scenarios

Typical questions a visitor might ask (used to guide content decisions):

  • What are the must-see attractions in Huancavelica if I only have two or three days to visit?
  • What cultural festivals or traditions can I experience in Huancavelica, and when do they happen?
  • How do I get to Huancavelica and what should I pack considering the weather and altitude?
  • Is Huancavelica a safe destination and what travel tips should I consider as a first-time visitor?
